01. About

I'm Eli, a Senior Java Engineer at Spice Factory Philippines (SFPH), where I've spent the past year building and maintaining backend systems for both web and mobile applications. As part of the Operations team, I design, build, and maintain services with Java and Spring Boot, focused on fast, secure, and high-quality delivery.

My day-to-day spans the full backend lifecycle — designing PostgreSQL schemas and automating migrations with Liquibase, implementing JWT-based security and following TDD, integrating messaging and caching with RabbitMQ and Redis, and wiring up third-party services for payments, SMS, email, and push notifications. I deploy via Docker and Heroku and keep production healthy with Papertrail, Sentry, and New Relic.

Across roles — from freelance work to leading teams at ITPI and RBS — I've grown deeper expertise in database optimization, cloud server management, production monitoring, and clean coding practices. Even in a fully remote setup, I enjoy leading technical discussions, offering direction, and owning key features end to end.

Outside of work, you'll find me watching anime, gaming on PC and mobile, serving in the church choir, and staying active through sports.
