HomeBell Privacy Policy

Effective October 9, 2026 · HomeBell is an open-source Android app by Elijah Gabriel Centeno.

HomeBell lets a family ring each other's phones from the gate, send "on my way" heads-ups, and see who answered. It is designed to collect as little as possible: your family sees only the nickname you choose.

HomeBell does not collect your location, contacts, photos, messages, or phone number, shows no ads, and never sells or shares data for advertising.

Sign-in

You sign in with your Google account through Firebase Authentication (a Google service). As part of signing in, Firebase keeps your Google account's email address and name; HomeBell's own server stores only the random user ID and the nickname you type.

Who processes the data

  • Google Firebase: sign-in, push notifications (Cloud Messaging) and crash reports (Crashlytics).
  • Render: runs the HomeBell server (Singapore region).
  • Neon: hosts the database (Singapore region).

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Only you and members of your family can see your family's data in the app.

Phone permissions

  • Notifications and full-screen alerts: so a ring can wake a locked phone, like an incoming call.
  • Ignore battery optimizations: so Android doesn't delay rings to save battery.
  • Do Not Disturb access (optional, off by default): only if you turn on "Ring even on Silent / Do Not Disturb".

Deleting your data

In the app: Settings → Delete my account. This removes you from your family, forgets your phone and heads-ups, replaces your nickname in past activity with "Former member", and deletes your HomeBell sign-in.

Without the app: email mrelijahcenteno@gmail.com with the subject "HomeBell account deletion" and the nickname and family name you used. Requests are handled within 30 days.

You can also just leave a family (Settings → Leave family) and keep your account.

Children

HomeBell is meant for families. Children should use it with a parent's or guardian's permission; it collects no more from them than from anyone else.
